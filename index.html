RefreshMate is an auto refresh extension for Chrome and Edge that reloads a page on a schedule you choose, from a few seconds to several hours. Set an interval, start it and let the page stay current while you work on something else. A small overlay shows the countdown, refresh progress and a pause button, and the toolbar badge keeps the timer visible when the overlay is hidden. No account, no ads and no tracking. Built for dashboards, queues, listings and any page that only updates when you reload it.

01

Everything it does

The core features at a glance.

01

Presets for 5 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute and 5 minutes, plus a custom interval in minutes and seconds.

02

Hard refresh bypasses the cache when a page keeps serving an old copy.

03

A floating overlay shows the countdown, progress and a pause or resume control.

04

The toolbar badge shows the time to the next refresh.

05

Auto-pause while you type in an input field, so forms are not wiped mid-entry.

06

CAPTCHA detection stops the refresh and tells you why; resume when you are ready.

Also included

  • A maximum refresh count stops the loop after the number you set; leave it empty for unlimited.
  • Scroll position is restored after each reload.
  • Per-site settings: every domain keeps its own interval, hard-refresh choice and limits.
  • Six interface languages.

02

Free vs Premium

The free tier is a complete tool, not a trial. Premium is optional.

Free

presets from 5 seconds to 5 minutes, custom intervals, hard refresh, the floating overlay with countdown, progress and pause, auto-pause while typing, CAPTCHA detection, maximum refresh count, scroll restore, per-site settings, the toolbar badge and all six interface languages. One site runs at a time, and one matching tab is reloaded per cycle.

Premium

several sites and every matching tab at once, random interval ranges, keyword and regular expression alerts, any-change detection, notification sounds and the choice to continue after an alert. One licence key activates on two browsers, and removing the licence from one browser frees that slot again.

The licence is optional. RefreshMate keeps working offline and asks the licence server for a check about every two hours; if the check fails, nothing is locked.

Private by design

Your data stays on your device. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Read exactly what is stored and when the extension uses the network.
